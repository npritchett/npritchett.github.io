Family & Pride

The newly established North Carolina A&T Run Club is dedicated to fostering a strong sense of community through running. Open to students of all skill levels, the club aims to promote health, wellness, and teamwork. Whether you're a seasoned runner or just starting out, the Aggie Run Club provides a welcoming space to improve your fitness and meet new friends. Join us as we hit the track and roads to achieve our goals together. AGGIE PRIDE!

Upcoming Events

Join us for our regular Tuesday evening run at 6 PM! We're also excited to announce the "Aggies on the Move" 5K happening next month, a community run designed for all fitness levels. In addition, don't miss our special trail run event in November, where we'll explore the local greenway trails. Stay tuned for more details and make sure to lace up your shoes and be part of the Aggie Run Club!
