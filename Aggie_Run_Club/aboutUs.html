About Aggie Run Club

The Aggie Run Club is a vibrant community of passionate runners dedicated to promoting fitness and camaraderie among students, alumni, and friends of North Carolina A&T State University. Founded in 2010, our club welcomes runners of all skill levels, from beginners to seasoned marathoners. Our goal is to create an inclusive environment where everyone can enjoy the benefits of running while forming lasting friendships.

Each week, we host group runs, training sessions, and various events that not only enhance our members' running skills but also encourage a healthy lifestyle. Our members participate in local races, charity events, and even national competitions, representing North Carolina A&T with pride. We believe that running is not just a sport; it's a way to build a supportive community.

Our Mission

At the Aggie Run Club, our mission is to foster a love for running while providing opportunities for personal growth, teamwork, and community service. We aim to promote physical fitness, mental well-being, and social interaction through regular running events and training programs. By encouraging participation in various races and charitable events, we hope to make a positive impact on both our members and the broader community.

Join Us

Whether you’re an experienced runner or just starting, the Aggie Run Club invites you to join us for fun runs, social gatherings, and community outreach initiatives. Together, we can achieve our fitness goals while having a great time and making a difference in the community. Come be a part of our family, and let's hit the pavement together!
